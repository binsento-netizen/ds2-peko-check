Peko-Check Lv1See your Death Stranding 2 progress straight from your save. It is read in this browser, never uploaded and never changed.

How to read the orders. Done: delivered. In progress: accepted, not delivered yet. Available: you can take it at a terminal. Locked: not offered yet. Compare with… shows what changed between two saves, with a report you can copy, for example to send a test result (see “How you can help” on GitHub).
Order and item names are the game's own English text, read from the game files with ShadelessFox's Odradek. Item icons from game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0). Guide links go to Game8 or the Death Stranding Wiki. Source code, how to help and the roadmap: github.com/binsento-netizen/ds2-peko-check.
